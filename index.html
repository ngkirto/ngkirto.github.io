03 / PATH

Where I've worked

DEC 2024 – NOW

Software Developer · Votanic

Unity and C# on XRCC, the company's XR creation platform.

  • 20+ UI panels and their backends, built with the UX/UI designer and keeping views and logic separate: asset import, Sketchfab login, Ready Player Me over HTTP, PC/XR networking, keyboard, dialogs, and microcontroller setup and debug.
  • A pooled, recycled list view used by 8+ panels, and a custom backend for the open-source SimpleFileBrowser.
  • The BLE device layer that lets several ESP32 and Micro:bit controllers drive XR scenes at once, and its cross-platform rewrite, BLE 2.
  • Contributions to XRCC's behaviour, upgrade, history and remote-trigger systems.
  • Five beginner tutorials that take non-technical users from zero to building in XRCC within a month. I learned XRCC from scratch myself, built each project from its behaviours and existing features with my own 3D models and materials (so there are no copyright issues), wrote step-by-step documentation for every tutorial, and helped update the XRCC tutorial website.
  • A 100-slide XRCC walkthrough for about 30 non-technical hospital staff, and two internal microcontroller labs for colleagues.
JUN 2023 – OCT 2024

Co-Founder · Metaprint

Virtual campuses, a large-format DLP print service, and tailor-made 3D and XR workshops for schools.

  • Walkable Oncyber campuses for two secondary schools, modelled from my own 360° photos and presented to principals and department heads.
  • Ran a large-format DLP printer for client orders for about two years.
  • 30+ workshop lessons for 150+ students, each designed around the school's equipment and goals and ending with take-home student work.
  • A 5-lesson Among Us workshop for Form 1–2 students: meshing, shading, rigging and posing a character in Blender, then making it printable. Each student took home their own print and painted it with acrylics.
  • A STEM cable car workshop kit for Ngong Ping 360: laser-cut plywood towers and 3D-printed cabins on a cotton-thread loop, driven by a small motor and battery. Over a month of client emails I revised the design several times to fit the budget, set up bulk orders for the laser-cut wood from Taobao, and quality-checked and reworked every motor before delivery; it also taught me how much packaging shapes a product.
  • A Fusion 360 NFC mahjong workshop for 6 HKUST students, and a 10-lesson campus-modelling pack for a partner to teach.
OCT 2022 – MAR 2023

3D Engineer Intern · Metamory

Blender visualisation for clients and tailor-made prints with sanding and spray painting.

SEP 2020 – MAY 2022

Student Technical Advisor · HKUST

Engineering workshops, power-tool safety videos, and AutoCAD layouts for laser-cut decorations. Taught myself Blender, then assisted a 4-day Blender workshop.
