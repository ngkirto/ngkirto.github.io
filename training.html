Lessons and tutorials

Courses and tutorials I made for STEM classes: two courses for Lam Tai Fai College at Metaprint, one in Blender built on the school's own rooms and one in character motion capture, plus the power-tool safety videos from the HKUST workshop.

    COURSE · METAPRINT · LAM TAI FAI COLLEGE

    LTFC Blender course

    10 LESSONS · SLIDES AND STARTER FILES

    Students learn Blender by rebuilding their own school: the classroom shape, table and chair, UV mapping, arrays, a metaverse scene, lighting, rendering, procedural textures and baking, then decorating the new gym and the bio lab.

    See the lessons and the school
    COURSE · METAPRINT · LAM TAI FAI COLLEGE

    Character Motion Capture course

    10 LESSONS · SLIDES, STUDENT HANDBOOK, TUTOR VIDEOS

    Students design their own character in VRoid Studio, film themselves, turn the video into animation with motion capture, then repair the model for 3D printing and take it into the metaverse.

    1. Introduction to VRoid Studio
    2. Hairstyle
    3. Cloth
    4. Motion Capture Video Guideline
    5. Motion Capture
    6. Animation Retargeting
    7. Repair 3D printable Model
    8. Export the character with animation
    9. Explore Metaverse
    10. Sharing Session

    The course comes with lesson slides, a bilingual student handbook, and a 10-part video series I recorded to train the tutors. They are internal material, so they aren't shown here. Available on request.
